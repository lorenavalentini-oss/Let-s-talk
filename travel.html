2 · Words to talk

Useful language, not a vocabulary test.

Use these expressions if they help you say what you actually want to say.

go with the flow

to accept changes and adapt without getting stressed

off the beaten track

away from the most popular tourist places

travel light

to take very little luggage

a hidden gem

a great place that isn't widely known

culture shock

the surprise or discomfort of experiencing a very different culture

worth the trip

good enough to justify the time, effort or cost

3 · Let's talk

Pick. Answer. Follow up.

Conversation card
Are you the same traveller today that you were ten years ago?
Try: From my perspective… · It depends on… · I've never really thought about that. · Something similar happened to me. · What makes you say that? · Would you do it differently now?
4 · What would you do?

A dilemma.

Your flight is cancelled and the next one leaves tomorrow. The airline offers a hotel, but you'll miss the first day of your trip. How do you react?
5 · Tell us the story

Make it personal.

Tell us about a time when…
…a trip didn't go according to plan.
Set the scene → What happened? → What did you do? → How did it end? → Looking back, what do you think now?
