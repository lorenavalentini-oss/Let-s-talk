2 · Words to talk

Language for life in progress.

Not a vocabulary test — just useful language when you want to talk about change, priorities and plans.

a lot on my plate

to have many things to deal with at the same time

go through a phase

to experience a temporary period or stage

look forward to

to feel happy or excited about something in the future

get the hang of something

to begin to understand or become good at something

make time for

to deliberately create space in your schedule for something important

a turning point

a moment when a situation begins to change significantly

3 · Let's talk

Pick. Answer. Follow up.

Conversation card
What's something about your life that feels different from a year ago?
Try: From my perspective… · It depends on… · I've never really thought about that. · Something similar happened to me. · What makes you say that? · Would you do it differently now?
4 · What would you do?

A dilemma.

You're offered an exciting new opportunity, but accepting it would completely change your routine. How do you decide?
5 · Tell us the story

Make it personal.

Tell us about a time when…
…you realized something in your life needed to change.
Set the scene → What happened? → What did you do? → How did it end? → Looking back, what do you think now?
